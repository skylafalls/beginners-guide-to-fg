# The post-n domains

Allow us now to introduce a new principle of THO: the **GvS conjecture**.
This principle simply states that _a generalized idea will always be more powerful
than a specific appliance of that idea_. This principle is our key to going beyond V◈.  
See, what could the practice of rFG be a specific subset of?
Well, the answer is simpler than it seems: **writing**.

This leads us to the _post-writing domain_, notated with the symbol **W°**.
**W°** is defined as _the collection of all objects too powerful to be represented in written language_.
This domain would genuinely be so powerful that no matter how much I time I toil away writing this,
I would never actually be able to describe it.
It is simply not bound by the natural restraints of human language and description.

At first, **W°** may seem like an impenetrable brick wall,
but THO-expansion reassures us that there _must_ exist a way to go past it.
By the GvS conjecture, we know that to do this, we must seek a notion even more generalized than writing.
Our key to this is in the idea of _potential_.
What follows from this is the _post-potential domain_, notated by the symbol **P°**.

P° is a domain covering everything beyond any form of potential whatsoever.
This includes the potential to transcend P°,
the potential to be completely unbeatable and even the mere potential to **be**.
At first, this may seem logically impossible to beat, but we must remember THO-expansion:
**_there is always a way_**.

THO-expansion tells us that given an indefinite amount of time,
every single definition we could ever make could be transcended,
no matter how transcendental it is.
We could attempt to delimit the performance of rFG,
the act of expressing in written language, the concept of potential itself,
and yet no matter what, we will **always** be able to go beyond in some way or another.
The object genuinely embodying this idea is known as **the Continuum**.
We shall notate it with the symbol **Ç**.
