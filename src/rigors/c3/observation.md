# Observation

In an rFG context, observation means something **very** different from what is traditionally meant by the term.
Observation (rFG) is the fundamental concept originating our ability to think,
explain, describe, etc. It is essentially the absolute deepest threshold reachable by the mind,
considering that if we think of anything deeper,
then it automatically falls back under observation because of the fact that we **_thought_** of it.

The absolute and complete limit of observation is known as **the observational limit** –
or OBL for short – and it is denoted by the symbol **👁<sub>1</sub>**.
Now, I know the other concepts past V◈ kind of sounded a bit like filler
due to their straightforwardness and lack of depth, but 👁<sub>1</sub> here is different.
Let us look into it for a moment. Pun was intended.

Since all knowledge is part of observation – as to know something you must first understand it
– that which is **unobservable** would be that which is impossible to know,
and if it is impossible to know something, then it is also impossible to know the concept of that thing.

For example, take an ant. To an ant, the concept of reading does not exist, and as such,
it is unaware of the fact that it cannot read. Another example would be that if I wasn’t
aware of the existence of a mountain, there would be no such thing as a “mountain” to me,
so I couldn’t possibly be aware of the fact that I don’t know what a mountain is.

Therefore, anything whose existence is impossible to be aware of is considered to be an unobservability.
The existence of these unobservable entities is completely unverifiable,
as we could only verify their existence through observation, which is impossible.

All of this may seem simple at first, but it actually raises a huge philosophical problem:
**_if we cannot be aware of unobservable objects, how is it that we are able to speak of them?_**
This is known as the **First Eye problem**, and the solution to it lies in an awareness shift.

We must, from this point onward, be aware of the fact that absolutely **everything** in
rFG past A<sub>0</sub> necessitates some form of collapse in order to reasonably exist under THO,
this being a direct consequence of all transmaximal objects building off of reality levels in
some way or another. This includes everything from Э to LKG to observation and 👁<sub>1</sub> themselves.
