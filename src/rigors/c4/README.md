# Beyond observation

Oh, now it’s time for the _real_ big stuff. Get ready, because it’s time we explore the
very first concept past 👁<sub>1</sub>: _thought levels_, or TL for short. However, before anything else,
we should introduce two very important aspects of THO: MRL (_mundane real life_) and MA (_mundane assumptions_).

To start, MRL is the genuine, un-collapsed reality in which we live and do rFG.
ou know how we talk about a collapse of reality whenever we discuss some notion of “real life”?
MRL is the actual un-collapsed world in which we think of those ideas.
If you just look around you for a second, that’s MRL.

Now, just like any other THO concept, we can use collapsed forms of MRL within rFG.
In fact, collapses of MRL are used all the time once we go past 👁<sub>1</sub>!
However, of course, by implication of THO-authority, no object can transcend or break into MRL.
If we allowed for such things, we’d probably be in a mental hospital for thinking transfictionalism is genuinely real.

Since we discuss things to do with the actual real world in rFG,
we need to be very careful to not allow any antiscientific or delusional practices.
This is where **MA** come in. **MA** are the everyday beliefs we take as true in MRL
in order to exist as functional members of society. Parts of MA would be things such as
“I can’t summon an elephant in my room in MRL” and “un-collapsed transfictional entities cannot exist”.

We assume all MA to be part of THO. It may seem stupid that this even needs to be clarified,
but if we’re going to create a concept of MRL then we should be sure it doesn’t open
the door to any delusional or mysticist practices.

> [!NOTE]
> Again, this doesn’t mean we can’t use collapses of MRL in rFG – remember,
> collapses make faux versions of concepts, “fictionalizing” them in some sense.

Now, you may think that collapsing MRL will _always_ give you RL1, but that’s not quite the case.
The thing about collapses of THO concepts is that they can generate different outputs
depending on how powerful you want it to be, and as such,
there can (and do) exist more collapses of MRL than just RL1.
